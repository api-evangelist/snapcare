# SnapCare

> SnapCare is an Atlanta-based healthcare workforce company — founded in 2017 as SnapNurse,
> relaunched as SnapCare in 2023 — that pairs a national per-diem and travel clinician
> marketplace with Booker, its SaaS workforce platform for hospitals, post-acute facilities,
> agency suppliers and clinicians. SnapCare acquired predictive staffing analytics vendor
> Medecipher in 2024 and merged with the nurse community connectRN in April 2026.

SnapCare sells to healthcare organizations, not to developers. As of 2026-08-28 SnapCare
publishes **no public developer program**: no developer portal, no API reference, no
machine-readable API contract (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Protobuf or WSDL), no MCP
server, no A2A agent card, no SDK in any public package registry, and no public status page.
Booker integrations are arranged through SnapCare's sales and implementation teams.
